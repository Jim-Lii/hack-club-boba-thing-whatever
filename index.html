Lindt Master Chocolatier

The picture above portrays a chocolatier in a white uniform carefully handling a liquid chocolate substance using a whisk

Below is another image of the same man, this time pouring the chocolate mixture onto a chocolate truffle.

The man is a character prevalent in Lindt commercials advertising their chocolate. Lindt is a Swiss chocolatier and confectionery company founded in 1845 and known for its chocolate truffles and chocolate bars, among other sweets.
